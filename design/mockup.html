Design concept · v3

Pulau Event,
brighter and simpler.

Now wearing the real brand name. Type is simplified to one clean typeface instead of a display/body pairing, and the sky-blue + sunset-orange palette from v2 turns out to fit "Pulau" (island) nicely on its own — blue for sky and sea, orange for the sunset over the water — without adding anything new.

What changed since v2
  • Branded as Pulau Event — the wordmark and product name are no longer a placeholder.
  • One simple typeface — Poppins throughout, replacing the Fraunces/Work Sans pairing.
  • Job feed gets a location filter — defaults to the freelancer's own saved location(s), shown as active chips they can adjust or add to. Automatic and adjustable, not one or the other.
